# Bhakti-latā-bīja · The creeper of devotion

An illustrated, animated journey through the nine stages of bhakti described by Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu 1.4.15–16), following Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu’s teaching of the bhakti-latā-bīja to Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī (Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta, Madhya-līlā 19.151–164).

The seed is planted in the garden of the heart, watered by hearing and chanting, protected from weeds and the mad elephant of offenses, and rises through the lokas, the coverings of the universe and the Virajā river, through the brahmajyoti and the Vaikuṇṭha sky, to Goloka Vṛndāvana, where it finds shelter at Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, flowers, and offers its fruit of prema.

**Open it:** https://visham108.github.io/bhakti-lata-bija/

- Use ← → (or the stage rail) to move between stages, Space to pause, S for the summary table.
- Turn on sound for the bansuri, temple bells and the sounds of each realm.

Based on the presentation *Bhakti-Latā-Bīja: The Creeper of Devotion from Śraddhā to Prema* by Vrndavana Hari Das.

## Structure

- `index.html` — the whole experience (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file; the eight covering textures are embedded).
- `art2/` — the paintings and sprites (`*_m.webp` are lighter copies used on phones).
- `art2/sfx/` — the soundscape.
